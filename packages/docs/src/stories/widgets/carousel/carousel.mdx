# Carousel widget

The carousel widget shows several presentations in a horizontal row, one page at a time, with **previous/next arrows** and **pagination dots**.

It has two layouts, set by the playback mode:

- **`modal`** (default): several items per page, as many as fit. Clicking an item plays the presentation in a modal.
- **`inline`**, **`inline-autoload`** and **`inline-autoplay`**: a single item per page, played in place, like the presentation widget.

The items are the same as the items of the [list widget](?path=/docs/widgets-list--docs): the thumbnail of a presentation and information fields like its title, publication date, publisher or any metadata.

## Basic Usage

By default, the widget uses the `modal` playback mode and shows the title and the publication date of each presentation. Here, the 12 presentations of a saved Smart Search: resize the window to see the number of items per page adapt.

## Recipes

### Inline: one presentation per page

The `inline` playback mode shows a single presentation per page, in the thumbnail aspect ratio, `16:9` by default. Clicking it replaces the thumbnail with the player.

<Canvas of={Stories.Inline}/>

### Inline: load or play the current presentation

With `inline-autoload`, the player of the current page is loaded, and the user starts the playback. With `inline-autoplay`, it starts playing as soon as the page is shown. Changing the page removes the previous player once the slide is over, and loads the next one.

<Canvas of={Stories.InlineAutoplay}/>

<Tip title="Autoplay">Browsers may block the autoplay of a presentation with sound. The example above is muted with the `volume` player parameter.</Tip>

### Hide the arrows or the dots

`navigation.arrows` and `navigation.dots` hide either one. Without arrows, the pages are changed with the dots, or by swiping on a touch screen.

<Canvas of={Stories.DotsOnly}/>

Without dots, only the arrows are shown.

<Canvas of={Stories.ArrowsOnly}/>

### Choose the information fields

The fields are placed above (`top`) or below (`bottom`) the thumbnail; the carousel has no `left` and `right` slots. Each field shows its default label, except the title and the summary; property `label` hides it (`false`), shows it (`true`) or replaces it (a string).

<Canvas of={Stories.InfoFields}/>

### Limit the items per page

In the `modal` playback mode, a page shows as many items as fit, at least `minItemWidth` wide. With a number, `columns` is the maximum number of items per page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Columns}/>

### Style the carousel

Besides the items, the arrows and the dots can be styled and placed: the arrows `outside` the items (default) or `overlay` over their edges with `arrows.position`, and the dots at the `bottom` (default) or at the `top` with `dots.position`. Like the play icon with `playIconUrl`, the arrows can be your own images with `previousIconUrl` and `nextIconUrl`: they replace the default buttons and fill `arrows.width` and `arrows.height`. Here, white tiles with custom play and arrow icons, bigger dots above them, and a slower slide.

<Canvas of={Stories.Styling}/>

And a single presentation per page, with square red arrows, small red dots and a square play button.

<Canvas of={Stories.StylingInline}/>

The custom arrows of the first example can also go over the edges of the items, with `arrows.position: 'overlay'`.

<Canvas of={Stories.OverlayArrows}/>

### Handle the click yourself

`onItemClick` replaces the modal, or the inline player in every inline mode, e.g. to open the presentation in a page of your own application.

| Property           | Default  | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|--------------------|----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| borderRadius       | `0`      | The border radius of the widget container.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| height             | `auto`   | The height of the widget container.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| width              | `100%`   | The width of the widget container.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

#### Items

The items are styled like the items of the list widget: see `item.*` in its [ListWidgetStyle](?path=/docs/widgets-list--docs#listwidgetstyle), with these differences:

- `item.thumbnailGap` defaults to `8px`, as the fields are above and below the thumbnail.
- `item.thumbnail.width` does not apply: it is for the vertical layout of the list widget.
- `item.thumbnail.aspectRatio` applies to every playback mode, to the player of the inline modes too: all the pages have the same height. The player fits a presentation of another aspect ratio inside, with bars around it.

#### Dialog, Close Button and Not Found

These are the same as the presentation widget: see `dialog.*`, `closeButton.*` and `notFound.*` in its [WidgetStyle](?path=/docs/widgets-presentation--docs#widgetstyle).
